About

I'm Corey Spangler. I grew up in Cabot and graduated from Cabot High School in 2006. I am a citizen, not a politician — I hold a seat on the City Council, not a career in office.

The record, in short

  • Cabot City Council, Ward 1, Position 2 — serving since 2023; I sit on the Budget and Personnel Committee. Elected in a contested race in 2022, reelected unopposed in 2024, and reelected in a contested race on November 3, 2026 (3,648–2,909, unofficial final totals). My third term begins in January 2027.
  • Chair, Democratic Party of Lonoke County — since December 2021. (Lonoke County in full: the committee covers the whole county; Lonoke by itself is a city in it.) Also a State Committee Member of the Democratic Party of Arkansas since March 2021.
  • Founder, The Stoic Opposition — my cross-partisan project: not a Democratic project, not a Republican project, not a third-party project. The vision in five words: both candidates unbought.

Education and work

My day job is in health-insurance regulation — the kind of work where you learn to read the fine print, because the fine print is where the money moves. I am Director of Regulatory Operations at Centene Corporation (since April 2023). Before that: health care advisor at Arkansas Blue Cross Blue Shield; assistant field director at Working America; outside sales at the Arkansas Democrat-Gazette.

Education: A.A., ASU-Beebe; B.A., University of Arkansas at Little Rock (2016); M.A. in Interdisciplinary Studies, UALR (2019). I have also completed all three years of UCA's Community Development Institute — the hours behind the ordinances, learning municipal law, economic development, and institutional strategy.

How I got here, and how I work

Every resident has a voice. I will meet anyone who lives in Cabot, and I invite hearing views I do not share. I don't go along to get along; if I believe a vote is wrong, I vote against it and I publish the vote, lone dissents included. My working rules: never consider political expediency and only focus on finding the best answer to the question in front of you; always operate in good faith; if it is not true, do not say it; if it is not right, do not do it. I call that doing the opposite of politics.

I also make my own campaign materials — spray-painted shirts and signs, mismatched fonts. That's DIY politics: clean money as a practice, not a talking point, and a reminder that a person made this, not a consultant.

The Four Pillars

The Stoic Opposition is built on four pillars, and its imagery is the same imagery I carry tattooed on my arm:

⚖ The Scales — Stewardship over Sophistry 🏺 The Chalice — Dynamic Applied Synthesism 🗡 The Sword — The Plebeian Reclamation 🦉 The Owl — The Policy Laboratory

The owl carries the Roman numeral VI in its wing in the original artwork. The pillars, in order: (1) Dynamic Applied Synthesism — governance as an operational science; (2) the Anti-Politician Scorecard — grade leaders from Sophistry to Stewardship, not left to right; (3) the Plebeian Reclamation — serious people taking the vacant seats of local party committees; (4) the Policy Laboratory — an open-source library of model bills, so the words come before the ask for power.

— For Humanity's Sake.
