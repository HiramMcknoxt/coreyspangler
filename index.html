Corey Spangler

Fiscal Responsibility · Local Control · Privacy

I represent Ward 1, Position 2 on the Cabot City Council. I have served since 2023, and on November 3, 2026, the voters of Ward 1 reelected me. (Unofficial final totals: Spangler 3,648 — Roper 2,909.)

This site is not a campaign site. The election is over; the work is the point. This is where I keep the public record of what I have actually done in office — the ordinances, the votes, the reasoning — where I explain the philosophy I legislate by, and where you can tell me what you think before the next vote, not after it.

How I legislate: Deliberately.

That's not a slogan I tested. It's the filter I run every vote through: Does this spend taxpayer money wisely? Was this decision made here, by people who answer to Cabot? And does it respect people's privacy?
