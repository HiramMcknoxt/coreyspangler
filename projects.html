Side Projects

The council seat is the day's work, not the whole of it. These are the other things I build — each one, in its own way, the same method pointed at a different problem: publish the record, build the mechanism, let people check the work.

The Stoic Opposition

My political project and personal brand: a cross-partisan, anti-duopoly movement working inside the existing parties' own machinery — taking vacant local committee seats, passing binding clean-money rules, and aiming at a politics where both candidates are unbought. It is explicitly not a Democratic, Republican, or third-party project.

thestoicopposition.com · Prototype site (GitHub Pages) · Substack: "The Stoic Opposition — Applied Political Philosophy"

Premise Check

A faceless logic-audit channel under The Stoic Opposition brand: it takes debate clips and public arguments apart with formal logic — premises reconstructed, fallacies named, rhetoric autopsied. Episodes post automatically; I review after posting and can pull any of them.

YouTube: @StoicOpposition

The Cabot Lore Repository

A comprehensive public reference for Cabot, Arkansas — history, politics, and news: council agendas and minutes fully searchable (including pre-2023), legislative audits, photographs, topic pages, and an ask-a-question layer. The public record, indexed, so anyone can check what actually happened. It grows by auto-append: new batches publish as they're built, with the full history of every version kept.

cabotlore.org

The Lonoke County Lore Repository

The county-wide sibling of Cabot Lore, built on the area's newspapers — the England Democrat, the Carlisle Independent, the Lonoke Democrat lineage — preserving Lonoke County's history the same way: primary sources first, summaries and pointers for papers still in copyright.

Lonoke County Lore Repository (GitHub Pages)

Locally Sourced

A podcast project in development, built around Arkansas people and the work they actually do. Handle: @LocallySourcedArkansas.

Ataraxia Aerials / GaiusFPV

My aerial drone media work. As GaiusFPV I fly single-take FPV flights synced, takeoff to landing, to a full song — no cuts, no speed ramps. Fleet: DJI Avata 2, DJI Neo, DJI Neo 2, DJI Avata 360, Axisflying Manta 30, Meteor 65. I am studying for the FAA Part 107 remote pilot certificate — in progress, not yet certified.

YouTube: @AtaraxiaAerials · @GaiusFPV
